Privacy Policy

Advanced Management Solutions (AMSOL) Software Privacy Statement.

Our Core Privacy Promise: Advanced Management Solutions builds privacy-first desktop software. Our applications execute 100% locally on your machine and collect zero personal data, zero analytics, and zero background telemetry.

Effective Date: August 30, 2026 · Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Publisher Identity

This Privacy Policy applies to the software applications and web download services provided by Advanced Management Solutions ("AMSOL", "AMS", "we", "us", or "our"), including but not limited to:

  • Magic-Watch (Desktop Gadget Chronometer & Visual Alert Complication)
  • MagicMidi (Desktop MIDI Studio, AI Audio-to-MIDI & Falling-Notes Visualizer)
  • Magic PDF (Desktop PDF document manager, splitter and merger)
  • Magic Video (Desktop video transcoder, trimmer and audio utility)
  • Downloads Portal hosted at https://download.amsol.ca

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and security of our users. We believe desktop utility tools should perform their functions efficiently and transparently without monitoring your activity or monetizing your personal information.

2. Desktop Applications Privacy (Zero Telemetry Guarantee)

All desktop software published by Advanced Management Solutions operates under a strict Zero Telemetry & Local Execution Architecture:

  • 100% Offline & Local Processing: All application functionality—including time rendering, audio analysis, MIDI synthesis, PDF manipulation, and video processing—runs entirely on your local CPU and GPU. Your files, documents, audio recordings, and media never leave your computer.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not include third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics (e.g. Google Analytics, Mixpanel), or user profiling tools in any of our binaries.
  • No Background Network Transmission: Our desktop applications do not transmit usage telemetry, crash logs, device fingerprints, or IP addresses to any remote server during operation.
  • Local Configuration Storage: User preferences (such as window position, theme choices, or interval alert options) are stored strictly on your local disk in standard application data directories (e.g. %USERPROFILE%\.magicwatch\settings.json) and are never synchronized to any cloud database.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

To eliminate any ambiguity, our software applications do NOT collect, store, sell, or share:

  • Your name, email address, postal address, or physical location.
  • Your hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, or biometric data.
  • Content from your documents, PDFs, audio tracks, MIDI scores, or video files.
  • Keystrokes, mouse movements, or application usage duration.
  • Browsing history or data from other installed software.

4. Downloads Portal & Web Infrastructure (download.amsol.ca)

When you visit our public download portal at https://download.amsol.ca:

  • Web Server Access Logs: In line with standard internet security practices, our server (Nginx/Caddy) records temporary access logs containing the requesting IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, and timestamp. These logs are maintained solely for DDoS mitigation, rate-limiting, error troubleshooting, and server performance optimization. Server logs are automatically purged within 30 days and are never linked to personal identities.
  • No Tracking Cookies or Web Beacons: The download portal does not use advertising cookies, marketing pixels, or third-party analytics trackers.
  • Local UI Preferences: The website utilizes your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to remember your visual Dark Mode / Light Mode theme preference. No identifying information is stored.

5. Open Source Transparency & Code Signing (SignPath Foundation)

Advanced Management Solutions is committed to verifiable open-source software integrity:

  • Public Source Code: Our application build specifications and distribution repositories are openly maintained on GitHub at https://github.com/alaahadyhostinger/download.
  • Cryptographic SHA-256 Checksums: We publish SHA-256 cryptographic hashes for all distribution ZIP packages and binaries on our website, allowing users to independently verify that downloaded archives match the official build.
  • Digital Code Signing: Our executable binaries are digitally code-signed to provide cryptographic proof of publisher authenticity and assure users that the software has not been tampered with, altered, or injected with malicious payloads since publication.

6. International Privacy Compliance (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA & COPPA)

  • European Union (GDPR): Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), we adhere to the principle of complete data minimization. Because no personal data is collected or processed, no profiling or automated decision-making occurs.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA): We do not collect, sell, share, or monetize the personal information of California consumers or any other users.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): Advanced Management Solutions complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act. We respect the privacy of individuals and safeguard digital autonomy.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): Our applications and websites do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

7. Security & Software Integrity

We implement rigorous security controls to maintain the integrity of our software:

  • Automated containerized builds (Docker) deployed over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections with automated Let's Encrypt / Caddy certificates.
  • Strict security response headers including X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, and Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin.
  • Automated healthcheck monitoring and dependency scanning.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new desktop utilities or evolving regulatory standards. When updates are published, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. We encourage users to periodically review this document.
